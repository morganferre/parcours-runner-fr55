Prepare a route for the watch

Load a GPX file, check the preview, then copy the resulting text into the settings of the Parcours Runner app from your phone. Route only, without streets: to get the streets, use tools/prepare_route.py.

4Paste it into the Connect IQ app

  1. On your phone, open the Connect IQ app (or Garmin Connect › Device › Connect IQ Apps).
  2. Go to My Devices › Forerunner 55 › Parcours Runner › Settings.
  3. Tap Route, paste the text, then Save.
  4. Keep the watch connected over Bluetooth: the route arrives within a few seconds. On the watch, the app shows the overview until GPS is found.

These settings are only available if the app was installed from the Connect IQ Store: an app copied over USB cannot be configured from the phone. If the Route field is empty, the watch uses the route built into the app.

If the watch shows “Incomplete”, the text was cut off when pasting: choose fewer points and try again.
